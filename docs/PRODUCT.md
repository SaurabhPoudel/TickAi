# Product: research and positioning

## The market (September 2026)

**Voice capture is table stakes.** Todoist added Ramble (speech to structured tasks) in 2026 and TickTick's AI Mode added voice input. Alune, Codot, Day Flow, Nori and Saner.AI all turn a spoken brain dump into tasks. "Say it and it becomes a task" is expected, not a differentiator.

**End-of-day review exists, but it's typed and built for office work.** Sunsama's daily shutdown makes you decide what happens to each unfinished task (carry, reschedule or remove). It is a manual, desktop-first ritual for knowledge workers, and even fans skip it when it fires while they're away. Night owls complain that tasks roll over at midnight whether they're done for the day or not.

**AI check-ins are mostly morning plans.** Saner.AI scans your inbox and calendar each morning to build a plan.

**Grocery apps remember recent items, not amounts or timing.** AnyList and Bring! surface recently added items; none learn how much you buy and how long it lasts from what you say.

## The gap

Nobody owns the bedtime moment as a spoken, two-way conversation about personal life.

## Positioning

"The to-do list that helps you sleep." Close every open loop in about a minute, hear tomorrow's first thing, put the phone down.

## Target user

Everyone managing a personal life: errands, cooking, shopping, seeing people. Not project management.

## MVP scope (built)

- Voice or text brain dump that splits into tasks; timed tasks sync to Google Calendar
- Task kinds with extra behavior: shopping (lists with pantry suggestions), social (asks who and when), cooking (suggests ingredients when you're also shopping)
- Bedtime voice check-in with carry-over, repeat-postponement nudge and purchase capture
- Pantry memory with learned "usually lasts" intervals and running-low suggestions
- Streak shown as a moon, with grace nights
- iOS, Android and responsive web
